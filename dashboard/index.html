How the ranking works

What counts as pre-token

A protocol is pre-token when its snapshot record carries no token at all: no market cap, no CoinGecko id, no symbol. Centralised exchanges are not ranked — an exchange is not farmable on-chain — and neither are versioned pools (a name ending in a release, such as “Aera V2”), which are pools of another protocol, not a launch of their own. The watchlist is ranked in full; below it, the early radar carries the 160 largest remaining pre-token protocols under the $50,000,000 floor, so the board holds 200 rows. A staking or restaking protocol is ranked on the same terms as any other: its own token, if it ever launches, is the launch being priced.

What the farm plan is

Every row carries six steps, generated from that protocol's own record — its category, its chains, its TVL and its age — never from a hand-written list of names. The steps are a positioning plan: use a fresh wallet, take one real position, act weekly, hold it long enough to be counted in more than one snapshot, and claim only from a link the protocol itself publishes. No airdrop is ever guaranteed. Nothing here is a promise that a protocol will launch a token, and nothing here is financial advice.
